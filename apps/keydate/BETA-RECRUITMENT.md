# KeyDate Beta — Edmonton & Greater Edmonton Area

**Goal:** 50–100 real testers from Edmonton + GEA, and one honest answer:
*do people come back and keep saving?*

**Target metric (the Year-1 gate):** ≥30% of activated testers log a deposit in
**3+ distinct months**. Everything below serves that number.

**Link to share:** `https://keydate.ca`

---

## Why Edmonton-first is the right call
- **The date is actually achievable here.** At ~$530K typical detached (vs.
  Toronto $1.36M / Vancouver $1.99M), a normal income reaches a real keys date in
  a few years. The product's core promise *lands* — in Vancouver it can read as
  cruel. Best possible first market for proving the concept.
- **You know it.** Local credibility, local language, you can meet people.
- **Small enough to saturate.** 50 testers in one metro creates a community that
  feels alive; 50 scattered across Canada feels empty.
- **A credit-union-dense province** (Servus, Connect First) — your Year-1 B2B
  target lives here too.
- **Honest constraint:** Edmonton is not representative of Canada. Retention here
  will likely beat Toronto/Vancouver. Say so when you report results.

## Where to find them (ranked by effort:reward)

### 1. Your own network — first 10–15 testers, day one
The highest-conversion channel and the one people skip. Text 20 people directly.
Anyone 22–38 who rents in the Edmonton area, or their kids/friends/coworkers.
**Do not post it to your feed and hope.** One-to-one messages only.

### 2. Reddit — the strongest cold channel
- **r/Edmonton** (~large, very active) — the main prize.
- **r/PersonalFinanceCanada** — huge; strict on self-promo, so lead with value.
- **r/FirstTimeHomeBuyer**, **r/alberta**, **r/Sherwood_Park**.
- **Rules matter:** read each sub's self-promotion policy, message the mods first
  for r/Edmonton and r/PFC. A "free tool I built, looking for feedback, no
  signup-to-view, no affiliate links" post from a real local account usually
  passes. A drive-by link drop gets removed and can get you banned.

### 3. Facebook groups — where GEA actually talks
Search and join: *Edmonton First Time Home Buyers*, *Edmonton Buy/Sell*,
*Sherwood Park Community*, *St. Albert Community*, *Spruce Grove & Stony Plain*,
*Beaumont/Leduc community groups*, *Edmonton Newcomers*. Ask admins before
posting. Newcomer and young-professional groups over-index on future FTHBs.

### 4. Employers & institutions (highest quality per contact)
- **NAIT, MacEwan, U of A** — student/alumni associations, financial-literacy
  offices, grad-student groups. Many are 2–5 years from buying: perfect.
- **HR / benefits teams** at large Edmonton employers (City of Edmonton, health
  authorities, energy firms) — "free financial-wellness tool for staff" is an easy
  yes and gets you 20+ testers in one email.
- **Credit unions (Servus, Connect First)** — doubles as your first B2B
  conversation. Even a "no" tells you how they think about young members.

### 5. In-person / local
Coffee shops and coworking near downtown/Whyte Ave, NAIT/MacEwan campuses. A
small printed card with a QR code to keydate.ca. Low volume, high conversion.

## The follow-up that protects your data
Send to anyone who stops logging for 6+ weeks. **This is the most valuable email
you'll send**, because it separates *"the product doesn't work"* from *"logging
was annoying"* — two conclusions that look identical in a retention chart.

> Subject: Quick one — what made you stop?
>
> Hey — noticed you haven't logged in a while, which is genuinely useful info.
> One question, one line back: **what made you stop?**
>
> - Too much hassle to log manually
> - The date felt too far away / discouraging
> - Didn't trust the numbers
> - Life got busy, might come back
> - Something else
>
> No wrong answer and no follow-up sales pitch — I just want to fix the real
> reason. Thanks.

| 1 | Reddit (mods first) + 2–3 Facebook groups | 30–40 |
| 2–3 | Employers, NAIT/MacEwan/U of A, credit unions | 60–80 |
| 4 | Seed the community yourself — reply to every post. Ask for referrals. | 80–100 |
| 6 | First retention read + "why'd you stop?" emails | — |
| 12 | **The number that matters:** 3-month deposit retention | ≥30% |

## What to watch weekly (admin console)
- **Activation:** joined → actually built a plan (if this is low, onboarding is broken)
- **Deposit logging:** the north-star; distinct months per user
- **Bank-sync demand:** the Feature-demand tile — decides the Plaid question
- **Community:** posts by *real* users (the "KeyDate example" ones don't count)
- **Qualitative:** every reply you get. In a 50-person beta, 10 conversations beat
  any dashboard.

## Rules for yourself
1. **Talk to people, don't just count them.** At this size, conversation > analytics.
2. **Don't inflate the number.** 40 engaged Edmontonians is a better story than
   400 randoms who opened it once.
3. **Ship fixes fast and tell testers you did.** "You said X, I fixed it" is how a
   beta group becomes advocates.
4. **Never claim more than you have.** If retention is 18%, report 18% — and what
   you're changing.
